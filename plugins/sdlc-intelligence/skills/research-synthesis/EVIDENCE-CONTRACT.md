# Research Evidence Contract

For each source record:

```yaml
source_id:
type: interview | survey | support | analytics | observation | document
location:
collected_at:
population_or_segment:
method:
authority:
limitations:
```

For each finding record:

```yaml
finding_id:
observation:
interpretation:
source_ids:
affected_segment:
frequency_or_prevalence:
impact:
confidence:
conflicting_evidence:
limitations:
implications:
```

Do not turn an implication into an approved product decision.
