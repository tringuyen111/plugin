# Social Visuals

Create platform-specific social visuals from a clear art-direction brief, project brand tokens, and host-native creative generation.

## Workflow
1. Resolve platform dimensions and safe zones.
2. Use `ui-ux-pro-max` for visual direction and `design-system` for brand/tokens.
3. Define headline hierarchy, focal subject, composition, CTA, and crop-safe area.
4. Generate the visual with host-native creative tools.
5. Inspect the rendered/exported result at target dimensions before delivery.

Keep critical content inside safe zones, use no more than two typefaces, preserve contrast, and avoid dense copy that competes with the focal subject.
