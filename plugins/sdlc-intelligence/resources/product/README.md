# Product

Product discovery and learning skills. Product owns outcomes, priority, and
measurement; it does not own detailed behavior, visual design, or technical
implementation.

## User-invoked

- **[product-discovery](../../skills/product-discovery/SKILL.md)** — Turn an idea, complaint, or weak signal into an evidence-grounded opportunity and next learning decision.

## Model-invoked

- **[research-synthesis](../../skills/research-synthesis/SKILL.md)** — Synthesize an existing product-evidence corpus into traceable findings without collecting missing primary evidence or hiding disagreement.

## Additional user-invoked

- **[product-definition](../../skills/product-definition/SKILL.md)** — Define outcomes, metrics, priority, and epic scope from an approved opportunity.
- **[accept-uat](../../skills/accept-uat/SKILL.md)** — Record the authorized Product/business acceptance decision for a fixed candidate and evidence package.

## Additional model-invoked

- **[metrics-review](../../skills/metrics-review/SKILL.md)** — Review product performance with explicit metric definitions, caveats, and non-causal interpretation.
