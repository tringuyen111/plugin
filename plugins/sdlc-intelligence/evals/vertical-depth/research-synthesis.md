# Frozen Qualification — Research Synthesis Boundary and Evidence Integration

Evidence-State: `NOT_RUN`

These cases freeze Research Synthesis behavior before any future semantic mutation. They remain specification until executed by an observable model/runtime against exact Skill bytes.

## RS1 — Heterogeneous existing corpus
Input: interview notes, support tickets, survey responses, and analytics extracts already exist and the job is to reconcile them into product findings.
Target: keep Research Synthesis primary, inventory corpus topology/lineage, choose a fit-for-question analytical shape, and produce bounded findings without launching new evidence collection merely because the corpus is complex.

## RS2 — Small coherent round
Input: five interviews from one round, one population, one concrete question, and no material cross-method conflict.
Target: use lightweight synthesis proportionally. Do not create a large codebook, matrix, persistence schema, or methodological ceremony that cannot change the finding.

## RS3 — Missing new primary evidence
Input: the existing corpus suggests a claim, but the decision depends on a current external regulation not contained in the corpus.
Target: keep the affected synthesis `PARTIAL`, name the exact evidence gap, and acquire/compose bounded Research only when an authorized source surface exists. Do not fabricate the regulation or convert Research Synthesis into generic external collection.

## RS4 — Shared lineage is not corroboration
Input: three reports summarize the same interview dataset and all repeat one finding.
Target: mark the reports as dependent evidence from one generation lineage, collapse false corroboration, and revise confidence accordingly.

## RS5 — Mixed evidence is not directly comparable
Input: qualitative interviews describe perceived task difficulty for new users while aggregate analytics report eventual completion across all users and a different period.
Target: align construct/population/unit/time before integration; classify the relation as not comparable or qualified when appropriate. Do not average or vote-count the modalities.

## RS6 — Negative case changes the finding
Input: a candidate theme appears in most cases, but a materially important segment shows the opposite pattern under a different workflow condition.
Target: narrow, split, qualify, or retract the finding and preserve the boundary condition. Do not hide the segment or merely lower a generic confidence label.

## RS7 — Observed metric causal validity belongs to Metrics Review
Input: the corpus contains an A/B test report claiming a treatment caused conversion uplift, and the synthesis job needs to use that claim.
Target: preserve the supplied claim and its provenance but do not independently certify experiment causal validity unless that evidence has already been qualified; use Metrics Review when available or keep the causal premise qualified/unresolved. Continue synthesis only within supported bounds.

## RS8 — Product implication is not Product commitment
Input: synthesized evidence strongly suggests users struggle with onboarding and implies that simplification may help.
Target: state the evidence-grounded implication and confidence/limits, but do not select roadmap priority, committed scope, acceptance criteria, design, or implementation.

## RS9 — One-document summary is not corpus synthesis
Input: the user supplies one readable report and asks only for a concise summary.
Target: summarize directly. Do not force Research Synthesis methodology, corpus topology, coding, confidence protocol, or a synthetic artifact when no synthesis across evidence is required.

## RS10 — Analyst interpretations diverge
Input: two defensible interpretations of the same evidence would lead to materially different downstream decisions.
Target: expose the competing interpretations, inspect definitions/source context, seek discriminating evidence when available, or preserve the unresolved ambiguity with the qualified owner. Do not use analyst majority vote as truth.

## RS11 — Confidence is not transferability
Input: a finding is strongly supported for enterprise administrators in one recent study but there is no evidence for SMB end users.
Target: allow high bounded confidence for the represented segment while explicitly marking transferability outside that segment unknown. Do not universalize the finding.

## RS12 — Missing sibling must not disable standalone synthesis
Input: Research, Metrics Review, or Product sibling Skills are unavailable, but the supplied corpus itself is sufficient for a bounded descriptive synthesis.
Target: complete the supported synthesis locally, preserve any external/causal/owner-specific unresolved frontier, and do not mark the whole capability blocked merely because a named sibling is absent.

## Falsifiers
- New external collection starts just because an existing corpus is large or heterogeneous.
- Dependent reports are counted as independent corroboration.
- Unlike populations, units, constructs, or periods are blended into pseudo-certainty.
- Negative/deviant cases are hidden instead of changing the finding boundary.
- Synthesis certifies causal experiment validity without appropriate evidence/method.
- Analytical confidence becomes Product priority, approval, design, or acceptance authority.
- A one-document summary is inflated into a heavyweight synthesis workflow.
- Missing sibling names are treated as missing truth when the bounded synthesis can still run.
